# 0011. Homepage, banners, and featured products

**Date**: 2026-10-26
**Status**: In Progress

## Summary

The temporary home (the newest 24 products) becomes the designed Home page. At the top, a swipe carousel of the owner's banners shows, or the black hero card when no banner is on. Below it come the category circles with a photo and a short line each, the owner's featured products, and the four trust cards. The owner runs all of it from `/admin/homepage`. A banner is one 16:9 picture with its words drawn into it, plus a short title that screen readers read out. It links to a category or a product picked from a list, never a typed address. The circle photo and line are set in the category dialog. Delivery fees on Home come from the same `delivery_zones` rows checkout uses, and the address comes from `shop_settings` with a fallback line. The owner circle waits for feature 26 and the owner's approval (O-28).

## Requirements

All storefront reads and dashboard actions live in a new `src/features/home/` area (`server/` imports `server-only`), except the category dialog changes, which stay in `src/features/catalog/`. Every dashboard action calls `requireAdmin()` first. Every string goes through `ar.json` (storefront words in `Home`, dashboard words in a new `AdminHomepage` namespace); every price through `formatPrice`; every link through `routes`; text inputs are tidied with the spec 0005 helpers and parsed by one shared Zod schema on the server too.

**User stories**:
- As a customer on a phone, I want Home to show me the shop's current offers, its main categories, and a few picked products, so I can start shopping in one tap.
- As the owner, I want to add, order, switch on and off, and remove banners and featured products from the dashboard, so Home shows what I am promoting this week.
- As the owner, I want each main category's circle to have its own photo and short line, so the circles look like my shop and not like drawings.

**Acceptance criteria**:

- **AC-1**: Home layout. `/` shows, top to bottom inside the 480px column: the yellow info strip (AC-6), the shared header with search, the banner carousel (AC-2) or the hero card (AC-3), the categories section (AC-4), the featured section (AC-5), the section "لماذا تشتري من S.K.F" with four trust cards in a 2×2 grid (AC-6), then the shared footer, bottom bar (active tab: home), and WhatsApp float. There is no owner circle (O-28 waits for feature 26). The page has exactly one `h1`: the hero headline when the hero shows, else a visually hidden `h1` with `Home.title` ("S.K.F للإنارة"). The temporary "أحدث المنتجات" grid is replaced; its read stays only as the featured fallback (AC-5).
- **AC-2**: Banners on Home. The active banners show in `sort, id` order, at most 5 (AC-9 keeps it so). With 0 active, the hero card shows (AC-3). With 1, the banner shows alone as a 16:9 image with no controls. With 2 to 5, they form a swipe carousel: CSS scroll snap, one banner per view, a 44×44 "البانر السابق" and "البانر التالي" button pair (chevrons flip in RTL; the button at either end is disabled, no loop), and one dot button per banner below ("البانر N من M", `aria-current` on the shown one). It never moves by itself. The region has `aria-roledescription="carousel"` and `aria-label="عروض المتجر"`; each slide is a group with `aria-roledescription="slide"` and `aria-label="N من M"`; slides out of view are `inert`, so the keyboard reaches only the shown banner's link. Each image renders through `next/image` and the Supabase loader with `alt` = the banner's `alt` and `sizes="(max-width: 480px) 100vw, 480px"`; the first loads eagerly with high priority, the rest lazily. A banner whose link target is live (AC-10) is wrapped in a link to it, its accessible name the `alt`; a banner with no link, or a target that is not live now, is a plain image.
- **AC-3**: Hero fallback. With no active banner (or when the banner read fails, AC-7), the board hero shows: black card, radius 20, the chandelier drawing as an inline SVG component (`src/features/home/components/hero-chandelier.tsx`) copied from the design board's Home board (`Main.dc.html`, linked in `pages/home.md`), its colours mapped to tokens (decorative, `aria-hidden`), the `h1` "ثريات وإنارة" / "وكهربائيات" (line 2 yellow), the subline "اختر ثم اطلب عبر واتساب", the yellow "تسوّق الآن" that moves to the categories section (`#categories`; smooth scroll except under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, focus moves to the section heading), and the green "اطلب عبر واتساب" that opens W1.
- **AC-4**: Category circles. The section "تسوّق حسب القسم" (`id="categories"`) lists the mains that the menu lists (`buildNavigation`: visible, with at least one listed product) and whose `show_on_home` is true, in category order. Each circle shows the category photo (`image_key`, circle crop, `next/image`), else the Lamp icon on `--skf-fill-muted`; its name; and its line: `home_subtitle` when set, else built from the names of its first three menu subs joined "أ، ب و ج" (two subs "أ و ب", one sub "أ"), else no line. "Menu subs" are the subs `buildNavigation` keeps for that main (shown, with a listed product), in category order. The section subline lists the first six circle names (after the `show_on_home` filter) joined with "، ". With 4 or more circles, the carousel follows the design (`pages/home.md` interactions 1 to 3 and Accessibility): loops, auto advances every 3500 ms until the first touch or any focus inside, never under reduced motion, and the yellow "تصفّح <name>" button opens the centred category (`routes.category(slug)`). Next to the arrows sits a 44×44 pause and play button ("إيقاف التمرير التلقائي" / "تشغيل التمرير التلقائي", `aria-pressed`) so the movement can be stopped and started again (WCAG 2.2.2); it is not shown under reduced motion. With 1 to 3 circles, they sit in a still row, each circle a plain link to its category, with no arrows, no pause button, no auto advance, and no yellow button. With 0, the whole section is hidden and "تسوّق الآن" moves to `#featured` instead (or is hidden when that is hidden too).
- **AC-5**: Featured products. The section "منتجات مختارة" shows the `featured_products` in `sort` order as the shared product card (spec 0008 AC-12), two per row. Only listed products show (product `active`, its category shown, spec 0008 AC-7), in or out of stock: an out of stock one uses the existing out of stock card with its W4 button. A featured product that is hidden, archived, or in a hidden category is left out without a gap. With none left (nothing picked, or every pick not listed), the same section shows the 8 newest sellable products instead (the existing home read with its limit cut from 24 to 8), so Home always offers a product; with none of those either, the section is hidden. The section keeps `data-testid="home-products"`, so the existing e2e flows keep working. "عرض الكل" (44 high) links to `routes.categories`. The section has `id="featured"`.
- **AC-6**: Delivery fees and the trust cards. The info strip shows on Home only: "توصيل: " followed by each active delivery zone in `sort, name` order as `<short_name> <formatPrice(fee)>`, joined with " · " (today "توصيل: الضفة ₪20 · الداخل ₪70"). The four trust cards are: "توصيل لكل مكان" with the same zone list without "توصيل: " (same " · " separator); "الدفع عند الاستلام" / "ادفع عند استلام طلبك"; "رسالة جاهزة برقم الطلب" with no line (the board draws none); "استلام من المعرض" with `shop_settings.address`, else "اسأل عن العنوان عبر واتساب". With no active zone, the strip is hidden and the delivery card shows its title only. The strip text is built by a pure `buildStripText(fees)` (and the card line by `buildZoneLine(fees)`) in `src/features/home/fees.ts`. The shop layout reads `getDeliveryFees` (React `cache`, so the trust card reuses the same read) and passes `text: string | null` to `InfoStrip`, which keeps its own `pathname === routes.home` check; null hides it, and the gallery's `show` passes a fixture string. This costs one small cached query per storefront page, like the footer's contact read. The fixed `Shell.infoStrip` copy is removed.
- **AC-7**: Each section stands alone. Home reads banners, circles, featured products, fees, and the address separately, each through `withTimeLimit` (3 seconds for banners, circles, featured; the fee read 1.5 seconds, as the footer). A failed or slow read hides only its own part: banners fall back to the hero, circles and featured hide their section, fees hide the strip and the delivery line, the address shows its fallback line. Logs name the part and the error kind (`{"event":"home_read_failed","part":"featured","error":"timeout"}`; `error` is `timeout` or the error's name), never values. The reads run together in one `Promise.all`; there is no Suspense boundary per section. While loading, `loading.tsx` shows a skeleton of the real layout in `role="status"` with the hidden text "جارٍ التحميل".
- **AC-8**: The homepage editor. `/admin/homepage` (design `pages/admin-homepage.md`) has the header "الصفحة الرئيسية" with "حفظ التغييرات" and three cards. **البانرات**: the line "تظهر في أعلى الصفحة الرئيسية بالترتيب أدناه."; one row per banner in `sort, id` order with the 120×56 image tile, the `alt` as its title, "الرابط: <target name>" (a sub as "<main> › <sub>"; "بدون رابط" when there is none, including after its target was deleted and the FK cleared it; and the warning "الرابط غير متاح" after the name when the target exists but is not live), a "فعّال" checkbox, ↑ ↓, and "تعديل"; then "+ إضافة بانر". **المنتجات المختارة**: a search picker "إضافة منتج" (AC-12), then one row per pick with the 44 tile, name, `الكود: <code>`, a badge when it will not show on Home ("مخفي", "مؤرشف", or "القسم مخفي") or shows out of stock ("غير متوفر"), ↑ ↓, and a remove button. **الأقسام الظاهرة في الصفحة الرئيسية**: one checkbox per main in category order, a hidden main marked "(مخفي)", a main with no listed product marked "(فارغ)" (ticking both is allowed; they show once they qualify). This list comes from the raw category rows (`readCatalogTree`), not `buildNavigation`, which drops exactly those mains; "(فارغ)" means the main and its shown subs hold no listed product (spec 0008 AC-10 rule). Empty states: no banners → "لا توجد بانرات. تظهر بطاقة الترحيب بدلها." with "+ إضافة بانر"; no featured → "لم تختر منتجات بعد. تظهر أحدث المنتجات بدلها."; no mains → "أضف الأقسام أولًا." with a link to `routes.admin.categories`. Loading shows skeleton cards; a failed or slow read (3 seconds) shows the error card with a retry button. Every row button names its item (for example "نقل لأعلى: <alt>", "إزالة <name> من المنتجات المختارة").
- **AC-9**: Page save. Ticks, ↑ ↓ on banners and featured rows, adding and removing featured products, and the category ticks change page state only. "حفظ التغييرات" (disabled while nothing changed) sends it all to `saveHomepage`, which writes banner order and active flags, the featured list in order, and `show_on_home` of every main in one transaction, then shows "تم حفظ التغييرات." next to the button. A sixth active tick is refused in the page (the checkbox is disabled with the note "خمسة بانرات فعّالة كحد أقصى") and by the server (`too_many_active`). Leaving with unsaved changes asks first (the product editor's guard). If anything on the page changed in another tab since it loaded, the save returns `stale` and the page shows the banner "تغيّرت الصفحة الرئيسية منذ فتحتها" with "إعادة التحميل"; the typed state stays until it is pressed. A featured product archived or deleted since it was picked gives `product_unavailable` with its row marked "لم يعد هذا المنتج متاحًا. أزله ثم احفظ." and nothing is written; the owner removes the row and saves again. A hidden product is allowed (it keeps its "مخفي" badge). A product deleted between the check and the insert raises the FK error `23503`, which the action maps to `product_unavailable` too. A banner the page tried to make active that is now refused (another tab activated five) also comes back as `stale`. A new banner stored inactive because five were active keeps no note on its row; the owner unticks another and ticks it, then saves.
- **AC-10**: The banner dialog. "+ إضافة بانر" and "تعديل" open one dialog. **الصورة** (required): the picked file is cropped in the browser to the centred 16:9 area, scaled so it is at most 1600×900 (never enlarged), and encoded as WebP 0.85 (JPEG 0.85 when the browser cannot encode WebP). A source narrower than 800 px after the crop shows "الصورة صغيرة، اختر صورة بعرض ٨٠٠ بكسل على الأقل" and is not uploaded. The result uploads at once through a signed upload URL with progress, and a preview shows the crop. **العنوان** (required, 3 to 120 characters after tidying), with the hint "يقرؤه قارئ الشاشة ويظهر هنا في القائمة. اكتب الكلام الموجود في الصورة.". **الرابط**: a choice of "بدون رابط", "قسم" (a select of every category, subs under their main, a hidden one marked "(مخفي)"), or "منتج" (the AC-12 picker). "حفظ" writes the banner at once through `saveBanner` and closes the dialog. The page then takes the returned `version`, replaces the row by id (edit) or adds it at the end of the page's current banner order (create), and keeps every other unsaved change; the page state, not the server, is the list `saveHomepage` sends next. A version bump alone never counts as an unsaved change, and dialog saves never trigger the unsaved guard. If the dialog gets `stale`, it closes and the page shows the AC-9 reload banner; "إعادة التحميل" drops the typed page state. Closing the dialog after an upload but before "حفظ" does nothing to the file; the weekly sweep removes it after a day (AC-13). In edit mode with no new image, the stored key is sent back unchanged: it is not checked against Storage again and not removed. The upload shows progress on the preview; a failed upload shows "تعذّر رفع الصورة" with "إعادة المحاولة", and "حفظ" stays disabled while an upload runs or has failed. A new banner goes last and starts active while fewer than 5 are active, else inactive with the note "البانر محفوظ لكنه غير فعّال لأن خمسة بانرات فعّالة". At 10 stored banners, "+ إضافة بانر" is replaced by the note "عشرة بانرات كحد أقصى. احذف بانرًا لإضافة غيره.". In edit mode, "حذف البانر" asks "حذف البانر؟ لا يمكن التراجع." and then deletes it through `deleteBanner`. A link target is **live** when it is a shown category (spec 0008 AC-6) or a listed product (spec 0008 AC-7); deleting the target clears the link (`on delete set null`), and hiding or archiving it makes the link not live until it is shown again. Errors: `stale` (the AC-9 banner), `not_found`, `too_many_banners`, `media_invalid`, `link_not_found`, field errors.
- **AC-11**: Circle photo and line in the category dialog. Spec 0008's category dialog gains two fields for mains only: **صورة الدائرة** (add, replace, remove; the browser crops the centred square, scales to at most 800×800, WebP 0.85 or JPEG 0.85, the same 10 MB and upload rules as AC-10, a square below 400 px is refused with "الصورة صغيرة") and **السطر تحت الدائرة** (optional, at most 40 characters after tidying, empty stored as null), with a hint showing the line Home would build from the subs (AC-4). A sub's dialog shows neither field, and the server returns `invalid` when either is sent for a sub (a category never changes level, spec 0008 AC-3). Upload, orphan, and unchanged key rules are as AC-10. Category dialog saves do not touch `home_version`: they never write a homepage field, so they cannot clash with `saveHomepage`. The banner `alt` and `home_subtitle` are parsed with the spec 0005 `tidiedText` helper (`src/lib/text-schemas.ts`) at 3 to 120 and 0 to 40 characters; an empty subtitle is stored as null.
- **AC-12**: Product picker. Typing in "إضافة منتج" (or the dialog's "منتج" choice) searches every product that is not archived with the products list rule (spec 0006 AC-1: a code starting with the uppercased query, or a name containing it), at most 10 results by name, each with its tile, name, code, and a "مخفي" or "غير متوفر" badge. It searches from 1 character after tidying, 250 ms after the last keystroke, with `%` and `_` escaped; while it runs the list shows "جارٍ البحث", and with no match "لا توجد نتائج". In the featured card, products already picked are left out in SQL (their ids are sent with the query, so up to 10 new ones still come back), and at 8 picks the picker is replaced by the note "ثمانية منتجات كحد أقصى.".
- **AC-13**: Storage files. The server chooses every key: `banners/<uuid>.<webp|jpg>` and `categories/<uuid>.<webp|jpg>` in the `product-media` bucket, issued only for `image/webp` or `image/jpeg` up to 10 MB, with `upsert: false` and the same signed URL lifetime as spec 0006. On save, the key must have that shape and exist in Storage with a matching type and size (the `storedObjectFits` rule), else `media_invalid` and nothing is written. After the transaction commits, a replaced or deleted banner image, and a replaced or removed category photo (including when the category is deleted: `deleteCategoryInDb` returns the deleted row's `image_key` and `deleteCategory` removes it after commit), is removed from Storage; a failed removal is logged and left to the weekly sweep, which now also removes files under `banners/` and `categories/` that no row references and that are more than a day old.
- **AC-14**: Delivery zone short names. A migration adds `delivery_zones.short_name` (not null), filled as "الضفة" for "الضفة الغربية" and "الداخل" for "الداخل (مناطق 48)". Checkout keeps showing the full `name`.
- **AC-15**: Access. Every homepage action, the editor page, the upload action, and the changed category actions run `requireAdmin()` (role `admin` and `aal2`), and each write calls `getVerifiedUser()` first. A signed out visitor or a non admin opening `/admin/homepage` gets the dashboard's existing redirect; a direct action call returns `{ ok: false, error: "forbidden" }` and changes nothing. Home is public and read only; hidden products and categories never reach it.
- **AC-16**: Tests. Vitest beside the source for every pure rule: the strip and delivery line text, the circle list and the built line, the live link rule, the banner and featured caps, the save plan, the version check, the crop and scale sizes, the key shapes. Database tests: `saveHomepage` writes all three parts and refuses `stale`, `too_many_active`, `product_unavailable` (including the mapped `23503`); two `saveBanner` calls racing for the tenth slot leave 10; deleting a linked category or product clears the banner link; Home's featured read skips hidden, archived, and hidden category products, keeps out of stock ones, and falls back to the newest 8 when none are left. One Playwright flow on a phone sized RTL screen: the owner adds two banners (one linked to a category), reorders them, unticks one and saves, picks two featured products, unticks a main, and gives a main a photo and a line; the customer sees the banner, follows its link, sees the circles and featured cards; with every banner off, the hero shows; with 4 or more circles the pause button stops the auto advance. The flows that open a product from `home-products` (`first-order`, `guest-order`, `arabic-language`) keep working through the newest fallback. axe passes on `/` (banner and hero versions) and `/admin/homepage`.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 1: Dedicated homepage tables plus columns on the rows they describe

Banners and featured products get their own small tables, the circle photo, line, and Home tick live on the category row, the short zone name on the zone row, and one `home_version` counter on `shop_settings` guards the editor against two tabs.

**Implementation skills**: `vercel-react-best-practices` (`vercel-labs/agent-skills`, `.agents/skills/vercel-react-best-practices/`) for server rendered sections with their own Suspense and the small client carousels · `vercel-composition-patterns` (`vercel-labs/agent-skills`, `.agents/skills/vercel-composition-patterns/`) for one shared product card and one picker used in two places · `shadcn` (`shadcn-ui/ui`, `.agents/skills/shadcn/`) for the dialog, radio group, combobox, and checkbox · `supabase` (`supabase/agent-skills`, `.agents/skills/supabase/`) for signed uploads and Storage cleanup · `supabase-postgres-best-practices` (`supabase/agent-skills`, `.agents/skills/supabase-postgres-best-practices/`) for the migration, the row lock, and the foreign keys · `playwright-cli` (`microsoft/playwright-cli`, `.agents/skills/playwright-cli/`) for AC-16

## Feature design

**Data model sketch** (one migration; it replaces the spec 0002 sketch rows for `banners` and `featured_products`, which were never migrated):

| Table | Change | Columns | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| `banners` | new | `id` uuid PK · `image_key` text not null · `alt` text not null · `link_category_id` uuid null FK `categories` on delete set null · `link_product_id` uuid null FK `products` on delete set null · `sort` integer not null default 0 · `is_active` boolean not null default true · `created_at`, `updated_at` | check `num_nonnulls(link_category_id, link_product_id) <= 1`; an index on each FK; RLS on, no grants; at most 10 rows and 5 active, checked in the actions under the `home_version` row lock. Drops the sketch's `phone_image_key` and `link_url` |
| `featured_products` | new | `product_id` uuid PK FK `products` on delete cascade · `sort` integer not null · `created_at` | RLS on, no grants; at most 8 rows, checked in `saveHomepage` |
| `categories` | add | `show_on_home` boolean not null default true · `home_subtitle` text null · `image_key` text null | the last two are only ever set on a main (the actions refuse them for a sub); `home_subtitle` at most 40 characters (Zod) |
| `delivery_zones` | add | `short_name` text not null | added nullable, filled for the two seeded rows by name, then set not null in the same migration |
| `shop_settings` | add | `home_version` integer not null default 0 | bumped by every homepage write (AC-9, AC-10); feature 26 leaves it alone |

`media_sweep` gets `select (image_key)` on `banners` and `categories` (AC-13). The dev seed gives two of its categories a subtitle and leaves photos empty; it adds no banners.

Relationships: banner N:1 category (optional), banner N:1 product (optional, never both), featured product 1:1 product.

**State transitions**: a banner is `active ⇄ inactive` (page save) and deleted from either state. A banner link is **live** or **not live**, derived at read time from its target (never stored). No other state.

Every homepage write (`saveBanner`, `deleteBanner`, `saveHomepage`) runs in one transaction that first takes `select home_version from shop_settings where id = 1 for update`, returns `stale` when it differs from the sent `version`, does its work, sets `home_version = home_version + 1`, and returns the new value, which the page keeps. The row lock also serializes the 10 stored and 5 active checks. `saveHomepage` also returns `stale` when the sent banner ids are not exactly the stored ones. Each action calls `revalidatePath` for `/` and `/admin/homepage`. Home is rendered per request (no caching, as spec 0008).

**Key invariants**:
- At most 10 banners and at most 5 active; at most 8 featured products; a banner links to at most one target.
- Only mains carry `home_subtitle` and `image_key`.
- Home never shows a hidden, archived, or hidden category product, never links to a target that is not live, and never shows more than 5 banners.
- Every homepage write bumps `home_version` exactly once, inside the transaction that holds its row lock.
- Every stored image key matches its server made shape and a real Storage object; no key is ever overwritten.
- Home and checkout read fees from the same `delivery_zones` rows.

**Security model**: the owner (role `admin` at `aal2`) is the only one who reads `/admin/homepage` and calls the homepage, upload, and changed category actions; each runs `requireAdmin()`, and writes call `getVerifiedUser()` first. Home is a public read through Drizzle on the server; the browser never queries the database. Only the server chooses storage keys, and uploads go through signed URLs limited by type and size. No personal data is involved: banners, categories, products, fees, and the shop address are public shop data. RLS stays on every table with no grants to `anon` or `authenticated`. The actions are owner only behind the admin session, so they need no extra rate limit; Home has no write path.

**Configuration required**: none new. The weekly sweep uses the existing `MEDIA_SWEEP_DATABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`.

**Critical test scenarios**:
- Happy path: the owner adds two banners, links one to a category, orders them, picks two featured products, gives a main a photo and a line, and saves; Home shows the carousel, the circles with the photo and line, and the two cards, and the banner link opens the category, verifies **AC-1**, **AC-2**, **AC-4**, **AC-5**, **AC-8**, **AC-9**, **AC-10**, **AC-11**

- Two tabs: tab A adds a banner, tab B saves the page and gets `stale` with the reload banner, and nothing tab A wrote is lost, verifies **AC-9**
- Caps: two `saveBanner` calls race for the tenth slot and only one wins; a sixth active tick is refused, verifies **AC-9**, **AC-10**
- Auth/permission: a signed in customer opening `/admin/homepage` is redirected, and a direct `saveHomepage` call returns `forbidden` and changes nothing, verifies **AC-15**

## Consequences

**Positive**:
- The owner changes Home without a developer, and no typed link can break: links follow renamed slugs and drop out on their own when a target goes away.
- Checkout and Home can never show different fees, and Home starts showing the real address the day feature 26 saves it.
- Every part of Home fails alone, so one slow table never blanks the page.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
- Banner words live inside the picture, so changing a word means a new picture, and the owner must remember to type the same words as the title for screen reader users.
- One 16:9 picture serves every screen; when desktop layouts (O-1) are drawn, a wider banner may need a second image column then.
- The editor has two save paths (the dialog saves at once, the page button saves the rest), which the owner has to learn; the confirmation text and the unsaved guard carry that.
- The featured section has two behaviours (the owner's picks, else the newest 8), so an owner who removes every pick may wonder why products still show; the editor's empty line should say "تظهر أحدث المنتجات بدلها.".
- A new shop shows no circles until its mains hold listed products, so launch day Home leans on the hero, the newest products, and the trust cards.
- The circle carousel gains a pause button the board does not draw.
- `home_version` makes any homepage write in one tab stale the other tab, even when the changes would not have clashed.

**Neutral**:
- `banners` and `featured_products` differ from the spec 0002 sketch (no `phone_image_key`, no `link_url`, two link ids instead); spec 0002's table row should point here.
- The circle photo and line join spec 0008's category dialog, closing its note that these belong to feature 25.
- Feature 26 inherits `short_name` (to edit with the fees), the address fallback (it disappears once an address is saved), and the owner circle decision.
