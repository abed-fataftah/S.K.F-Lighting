## Slice 9: Loyalty

### 31. Loyalty coins · needs a decision
Registered customers and electricians earn coins on orders, awarded only once you mark an order both delivered and paid, with earning rules you set in the dashboard.
**Done when:** coins appear only after an order is delivered and paid, the earning rule is editable, each coin change has a reason on record, and My Orders shows the coin balance.
